St. Madre Rae

Real and digital support to make your dreams come true.

A millennial mother raised inside American story culture who still believes wishes can become real.

I grew up in a generation surrounded by stories. Storybooks. Novels. Saturday morning cartoons. Live action. 2D animation. 3D worlds. Television. Cinema. Music videos. The internet. And now stories that arrive through the little screens in our hands.

I became interested in what those stories teach us to notice, want, imagine and believe is possible.

I use story, systems, culture, timing and patterns to help families and businesses make something better out of what they already have.

Rael Emard — Designer
